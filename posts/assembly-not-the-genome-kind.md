# Assembly (not the genome kind)

<!-- To add an image: ./add-image.sh path/to/photo.jpg -->

Coding in assembly gives me a chip on my shoulder that's hard to explain. There's a layer of abstraction between assembly and machine code, but I feel like I'm *talking* to the computer when it's assembly. I just don't get that bare-metal feeling when I'm coding up something in Python.
